Install it

Pilot builds aren't code-signed yet, so your computer will ask you to confirm once. That's expected.

Windows

  1. Open Otis-Windows-Setup.exe.
  2. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Otis installs for you only (no admin needed) and opens when it's done.

Mac

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Otis into Applications.
  2. Open Otis from Applications. If macOS says it can't check Otis, choose Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway next to Otis. Confirm with your password.
  4. You only need to do this the first time.

Connect Claude or Codex

Otis thinks with Claude Code or Codex, using your own account. Your usage counts towards your own Claude or ChatGPT plan.

  1. In Otis, choose Connect Claude (Claude Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise) or Connect Codex (ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Team or Enterprise). You'll also find it under Settings → AI engine.
  2. Otis installs the engine if it isn't there and opens the official sign-in page. Sign in and come back to Otis.
  3. When it's done, Home says which engine Otis is using.

Start with the sample workplace

On Home, choose Turn on the sample workplace (or Settings → Connectors → Sample workplace). It's a practice calendar, inbox and task list, so you can try everything without touching anything real.

For the first few days: please stick to the sample workplace and keep sensitive files out of your Otis folder. Once you're comfortable with how Otis asks before it acts, connect a real account if you'd like to.

Your first week

Five things to try.

These are the parts we most want to hear about. Try them in any order.

  • 1 · Something usefulTap Prepare for my next meeting or What needs my attention?, then ask for something you'd really want at work.
  • 2 · An approvalType Draft the reply to Lindiwe and send the email. Otis shows a card first. Edit the text, then Send.
  • 3 · CancelDo it again and choose Cancel. Nothing should be sent.
  • 4 · A documentTap Draft a document. It's saved to the Otis folder in your Documents.
  • 5 · RestartQuit Otis (from the tray icon on Windows or the menu bar on Mac), open it again and carry on where you left off.
  • Then: use it for realOver the week, come back to Otis whenever it might help. We want to know whether you did.

Feedback

Tell us how it went.

Two minutes, as often as you like. Something broken? In Otis, go to Settings → General and choose Report a problem. It saves a short file (no passwords or messages) that you can attach to an email to us.

What we look at

Install problems, confusing approval cards, the task you wanted most, and whether you came back to Otis. Short answers are fine.

Prefer email?

Email the Otis team and attach your Report a problem file if you have one.

Help

If something's not right.

Windows won't let me run the installer

Choose More info on the blue “Windows protected your PC” screen, then Run anyway. If your company blocks unsigned apps (AppLocker, Application Control or Defender rules), ask IT to allow Otis or deploy it with Intune, and let us know.

My Mac says Otis is damaged or can't be opened

First try System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. If macOS says the app “is damaged”, open Terminal and run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Otis.app, then open Otis again. This only happens because pilot builds aren't notarised by Apple yet.

Connect Claude or Connect Codex didn't work

Make sure you're signed in to the right Claude or ChatGPT account in your browser, and that your plan includes Claude Code or Codex. Company accounts may need an admin to allow it. Then try again from Settings → AI engine, and tell us what you saw.

How do I update Otis?

You don't need to. Otis checks for new pilot builds and updates itself. If we ask you to, you can also download the latest installer from this page and run it over the top.

Installing for a team with Microsoft Intune

Use Otis-Windows-Setup.exe from this page, installed per user with Otis-Windows-Setup.exe /S. Ask us for the full Intune guide, including how to pre-fill your company's Microsoft, Google and Slack sign-in details.

How do I remove Otis?

Windows: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Otis → Uninstall. Mac: drag Otis from Applications to the Bin. Your documents in the Otis folder stay where they are.

What can Otis see?

Only what you give it: your messages, the files in its Otis folder, and the apps you connect. Read the privacy policy.
